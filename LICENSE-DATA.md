## What this licence does and does not cover

**Covered:** the compilation — the selection, matching, structuring, version history and
cross-border barcode matching that make up this record. That work is ours to licence.

**Not covered:** the underlying recall notices themselves, which remain the work of the
publishing authorities under their own terms. Each register's publisher and licence are
recorded in `data/sources.json`. Some are public domain — a United States federal work,
for instance — and others are not. If you republish notice text at scale, check the
authority's terms as well as this one.

published and when it changed, not veterinary advice, and it holds no dosing, toxicity or
treatment information. A barcode that does not match anything here has not been shown to be
safe; it has only not been matched.
